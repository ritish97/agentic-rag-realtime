================================================================================
CORPORATE EVENT OPERATIONS & WEATHER CONTINGENCY POLICY
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: SOP-OPS-042
================================================================================

1. PURPOSE & APPLICABILITY
This policy governs all corporate gatherings, executive retreats, client summits,
and outdoor company events. All event directors and venue coordinators must evaluate
local meteorological forecasts 48 hours, 24 hours, and 4 hours prior to scheduled
commencement.

2. PRECIPITATION & RAIN THRESHOLDS
- Green Condition (Go): Precipitation probability < 25%, rainfall rate < 1 mm/hr.
  Outdoor operations proceed as planned.
- Yellow Condition (Caution): Precipitation probability 25% - 49% or light drizzle.
  Tent awnings and covered walkways must be pre-deployed; audiovisual gear must be
  water-shielded.
- Red Condition (Mandatory Relocation): Precipitation probability >= 50% or active
  rain forecast. Event coordinator must execute relocation to the indoor auditorium
  or designated pavilion at least 3 hours prior to guest arrival.

3. WIND SPEED LIMITS
- Sustained wind speed <= 25 km/h: Safe for all standard canopies, signage, and outdoor seating.
- Sustained wind speed 26 - 40 km/h: Inflatables and temporary banner towers must be
  lowered. Anchored high-peak tension tents permitted with reinforced weights.
- Sustained wind speed > 40 km/h or gusts > 50 km/h: IMMEDIATE outdoor shutdown.
  All activities must transition indoors immediately due to structural hazard.

4. TEMPERATURE EXTREMES & HEAT SAFETY
- High Temperature Protocol:
  * Heat Index > 32°C (90°F): Shaded hydration stations mandatory every 20 meters.
    Outdoor duration capped at 90 continuous minutes.
  * Heat Index > 38°C (100°F): Outdoor gatherings cancelled; strictly indoor operations.
- Cold Temperature Protocol:
  * Ambient temperature < 10°C: Industrial patio heaters deployed for outdoor receptions.
  * Ambient temperature < 4°C: Outdoor activities prohibited; transition to fireplace lounge.

5. DECISION AUTHORITY & NOTIFICATION
The Lead Event Coordinator and Regional Operations Manager hold unilateral authority
to execute weather contingencies. Notification must be broadcasted via company SMS
and email alerts no later than 2 hours before the start time.
